What changed?

FY26 Q2 revenue exceeded forecast by $7,172M (+6.9%), while operating margin compressed 271 bps.

Model bridge shows iPhone variance of $3,534M and Services variance of $2,007M.

The next question is why: installed base, upgrade timing, ASP, region mix, subscription attachment, platform monetization, premium feature adoption, payments, and promotion timing.

Public data does not isolate these drivers. The portfolio labels them as planning hypotheses, not reported Apple actuals.

Why did it change?

  • Installed base → upgrade rate → iPhone units → iPhone revenue
  • Installed base → subscription attachment → recurring revenue
  • Services mix → gross margin → operating income → EPS

AI-enabled feature rollout affects both sides of the tree: demand, subscription attachment, R&D, and infrastructure cost.

What would I do?

  • Recommend maintaining the current capital-return pace through Q3 while prioritizing AI investment within existing funding capacity.
  • Use subscription attachment upside as the first offset, with a visible gross margin guardrail.
  • Hold ARPU flat until public evidence improves.
